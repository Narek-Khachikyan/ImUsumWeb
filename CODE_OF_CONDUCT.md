# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want ImUsumWeb to be a practical, respectful project for people improving education software. Contributors and maintainers are expected to participate with patience, clarity, and care.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent, but accept corrections gracefully.
- Keep feedback focused on the work.
- Prefer clear evidence, reproduction steps, and concrete alternatives.
- Respect privacy and avoid sharing sensitive school, student, or user data.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Repeated low-effort disruption of issues, reviews, or discussions.
- Security disclosure behavior that puts users or data at unnecessary risk.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close issues, reject contributions, or block participants who violate this code of conduct.

To report a concern, contact the repository owner through GitHub.
